Each vase is one of a kind — thrown, glazed, and fired by hand in the Brisbane studio. Open a piece for its full story, dimensions, and materials. When a vessel finds its home, it's gone for good.

Shipping & packing

Every vessel is packed by hand with dense protective wrapping and shipped fully insured, Australia-wide. International shipping is available by arrangement — just ask.

One of a kind

These are singular art objects. Glaze movement, throwing rings, and small marks of the hand are part of each surface — no two vessels can ever be alike.

How buying works

Add a piece to your cart and send the order through — it's confirmed personally by email with card or bank-transfer payment, and your vase is taken off the shelf the moment you write.
